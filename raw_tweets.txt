标题: A human liver has been kept alive outside the body for a record seven weeks

正文: Researchers in Australia claim they’ve kept a human liver alive, on life support, outside the body for seven weeks, opening up speculative new possibilities in biomedical engineering. The feat, whose details are pending publication, was carried out in Sydney after a team led by surgeon and inventor Carlo Pulitano connected a donated liver to a system of automated pumps and sensors that fed the organ nutrients, supplied it with blood, and whisked away waste.&nbsp; In an interview, Pulitano, a transplant surgeon at the Royal Prince Alfred Hospital and a professor at the University of Sydney, likened the life support system to “an artificial body.” Its official name is the Extended Organ Perfusion System. “Our group is the only one in the world to have maintained a human liver under fully normothermic, physiological conditions for seven weeks,” he says.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Normothermic means body temperature, or about 37 °C. The previous record for keeping an internal organ alive outside the body was set in Switzerland several years ago. That organ—also a liver—lasted around two weeks.&nbsp; Most organs destined for transplant survive only a few hours in special ice buckets and need to be raced to their destination. Over the last decade, however, surgeons have been switching to perfusion systems, which pump warm or cold fluids into an organ. This can double the time it can be held before it must be transplanted.&nbsp; Pulitano says the key to breaking the record was to automate the systems used to keep the organ thriving. During a meeting in Switzerland earlier this year, the Sydney team described perfusing 55 human livers, one after the other. Some were connected to a system that needed to be adjusted manually, but for those placed on the automated system—which produced the seven-week survivor—the average survival time tripled, to about four weeks.&nbsp;&nbsp; Perfusion has already enabled doctors to restart hearts from dead people and keep them beating outside the body
