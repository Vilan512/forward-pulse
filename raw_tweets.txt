A number of influential models have found that this approach, known as enhanced rock weathering (ERW), could eventually remove 3 billion to 8 billion metric tons of carbon dioxide per year, helping to slow or one day even reverse global warming. An additional promise is that these applications of rock could help farmers by improving the quality of their soil. The analyses were led by researchers at CarbonPlan, a San Francisco nonprofit known for rigorous scientific analysis of carbon removal methods.
