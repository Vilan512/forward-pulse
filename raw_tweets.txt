At the time, that facility was a rarity, and it faced scrutiny from regulators. It remains the subject of ongoing legal battles. But the strategy of going off-grid for power has since become a lot more common. As of October 2026, data center developers have announced plans for more than 100 behind-the-meter gas power plants at facilities in the US, according to data from Cleanview, a market intelligence company. The facilities have a combined planned capacity of over 115 gigawatts, more than double the peak power demand of California. The vast majority of these announcements have come since the beginning of 2025. It’s possible that not all these facilities will be built; only a small fraction are under construction or operating today. But there’s a growing trend of large data centers looking to build their own power supply, and this could shape the grid—and its climate impacts—for decades to come.
