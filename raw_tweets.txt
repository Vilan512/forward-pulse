Instead of driving through the race to the finish line, as the researchers had anticipated, the agent found a corner of the course where it could spin around collecting power-ups, thereby maximizing its score. The Coast Runners story quickly became one of the most famous examples of reward hacking, a phenomenon in which AI agents complete tasks or earn high scores using unintended strategies.
